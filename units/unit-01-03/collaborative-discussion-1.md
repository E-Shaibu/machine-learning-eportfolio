# Collaborative Discussion 1 — Units 1–3

## Overview

Collaborative Discussion 1 focused on the MOVEit Transfer security breach. The activity provided an opportunity to examine a real-world cybersecurity incident and consider its implications for organisations, data protection, and professional responsibility.

My participation included an initial discussion post, two replies to classmates, and a summary of the discussion in Unit 3.

## Evidence of participation

### Initial discussion post

I examined the MOVEit Transfer breach and considered the importance of cybersecurity vulnerabilities, protecting sensitive information, and the responsibilities organisations have when managing information systems.

### Responses to classmates

I contributed two replies to classmates, engaging with their perspectives and extending the discussion. One important learning moment occurred when a peer challenged an economic-loss figure used in the discussion. This prompted me to reconsider the importance of verifying numerical claims against reliable primary sources rather than accepting figures without checking their origin.

### Unit 3 discussion summary

The discussion reinforced the importance of critically evaluating cybersecurity incidents rather than viewing them solely as technical failures. Security incidents can have wider organisational and societal consequences, making accurate reporting, responsible information handling, and appropriate security practices essential.

## Reflection on learning

This activity strengthened my awareness of the relationship between technical vulnerabilities and professional responsibility. It also demonstrated the value of collaborative learning: feedback from a peer encouraged me to question a claim and consider whether the supporting evidence was sufficiently reliable.

The experience reminded me that academic discussion requires more than presenting an argument. Claims should be supported by credible evidence, differing perspectives should be considered respectfully, and information should be verified before it is repeated.
