# Development Team Project — Airbnb Business Analysis

**Team:** Group 3 — Machine Learning  
**Report:** Airbnb Business Analysis Report

## Business question
Could Airbnb benefit from higher prices in selected New York City listing segments, based on the relationship between advertised prices and review-based demand?

## Data and methods
The report uses a 2019 Inside Airbnb New York City snapshot. It describes data preparation and feature engineering, K-prototypes clustering, and price prediction using a median benchmark, OLS, Elastic Net, Random Forest and CatBoost, with an 80/20 split and five-fold cross-validation.

## Findings
The report identifies differences in price sensitivity across listing segments. It recommends considering segment-specific pricing rather than applying one market-wide rule. The report notes that CatBoost achieved the lowest cross-validation RMSE among the compared price models, close to Random Forest.

## Limitations
Demand is proxied by review volume rather than observed bookings, occupancy or revenue. Advertised prices omit some costs and seasonal effects, and model error is larger for the highest-price listings.
